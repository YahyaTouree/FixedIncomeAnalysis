## Implementation Guidelines

### Selecting the Best Options
1. **Use actual metrics from your work** whenever possible
2. **If exact numbers unavailable**, use reasonable ranges (e.g., "15-20% improvement")
3. **Focus on business impact** and quantifiable outcomes
4. **Highlight specific technologies** and methodologies used
5. **Emphasize collaboration** and leadership aspects

### Customization Tips
- Replace percentages with your actual achievements
- Adjust project scope numbers to match your experience
- Include specific ML frameworks and tools you used
- Add any special recognition or awards received
- Quantify team size and collaboration details

### Resume Formatting
- Use bullet points with strong action verbs
- Lead with the most impressive metric
- Keep descriptions concise but impactful
- Ensure all metrics are believable and defensible
- Maintain consistency in formatting across all roles
